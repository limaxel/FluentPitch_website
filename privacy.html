Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 19, 2026

Welcome to FluentPitch! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience when using our AI-driven interview coaching application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use FluentPitch, we collect the following types of information:

A. Personal Information

  • Account Data: When you register, we collect your email address, full name, and optional profile picture (avatar). This data is securely managed through our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Profile Data: Any additional information you provide to customize your interview experience (e.g., target industry, experience level).

B. Device and Media Data

  • Camera and Microphone Data: FluentPitch requires access to your device's camera and microphone to conduct mock interviews.
  • Interview Recordings: With your explicit permission, we record video and audio locally during your practice sessions. Recordings are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers; they may be saved to your device's native Photo Library if you choose to export them.
  • Biometric & Behavioral Data: Our application analyzes your body language, eye contact, and posture during the interview to provide actionable feedback. This analysis runs entirely on your device. Raw camera frames, facial landmarks, and gaze coordinates never leave your phone — only aggregated summary metrics (for example, an eye-contact percentage per answer) are sent to our servers.
  • Speech: Your spoken answers are transcribed to text on your device. Only the text transcript is sent to our servers for AI feedback; the audio itself is not uploaded.

C. Purchases, Ads, and Analytics

  • Purchase Data: In-app purchases are billed by Apple; we never see your payment details. We receive transaction records (product, time, and an anonymised transaction identifier) via our purchase-management provider (RevenueCat) to credit your account.
  • Advertising: Optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers to deliver ads, subject to the App Tracking Transparency choice you make in iOS. Declining tracking does not limit any app functionality.
  • Usage Events: We record basic in-app events (for example, that a paywall or feature was viewed) in our own database — no third-party analytics service receives your usage data. These events are deleted with your account.

D. Usage Data

  • We collect standard analytical information about how you interact with the app, including session durations, feature usage, and error logs, to help us improve the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used to provide and improve the core functionality of FluentPitch:

  • AI Analysis: Your answer transcripts (generated on your device) and aggregated behavioral metrics are processed by our backend AI models to assess your performance and provide personalized coaching feedback.
  • Account Management: To maintain your profile, secure your account, and provide customer support.
  • Service Improvement: To analyze aggregate, anonymized usage trends to fix bugs and develop new features.

3. Data Processing and Storage

  • Third-Party Services: We utilize secure third-party infrastructure (such as Supabase for database and authentication, and Azure Web Apps for our backend APIs) to process and store your data.
  • AI Processing: Audio transcripts and performance metrics are sent to our secure backend servers to generate your AI feedback report.
  • Local Storage: Video recordings of your interviews are primarily processed and stored locally on your device unless explicitly uploaded or exported by you.

4. Permissions We Request

To function properly, FluentPitch requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera: To analyze body language and eye contact.
  • Microphone: To capture your verbal responses.
  • Photo Library: To allow you to upload a profile picture or save your recorded practice sessions.

You can revoke these permissions at any time via your device settings, though doing so will limit the app's core functionality.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption in transit and at rest, and secure Row-Level Security (RLS) database policies, to protect your personal data and interview histories from unauthorized access.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you — the app includes a free, permanent Export Data feature (Profile → Export Data) that downloads your history as JSON.
  • Request corrections to your profile data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account) — no email required. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.
  • Opt-out of non-essential data collection (where applicable).

For anything not covered in-app, contact us at support@fluentpitch.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:
