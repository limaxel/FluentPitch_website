Support

For help with FluentPitch, email support@fluentpitch.com. We usually reply within one business day.

Include in your message

  • the email address on your FluentPitch account;
  • what you were trying to do and what happened; and
  • your app version, shown at the bottom of Profile.

Do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, full payment details, or private interview recordings.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in FluentPitch if a previously purchased pass is not shown. Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your account and data

You can download your server-held account data from Profile → Export Data. To permanently erase your account and associated server-held data, use Profile → Delete Account.

Before contacting support

  • Confirm that FluentPitch has Camera and Microphone access in iPhone Settings.
  • Check your internet connection and reopen the app.
  • For purchase issues, wait a moment and use Restore Purchases once.
